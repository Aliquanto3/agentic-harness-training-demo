# Reporting and handling security incidents at Exemplia

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this procedure describes no real company. -->

A security incident is any event that threatens the confidentiality, integrity or availability of Exemplia's information: a phishing email opened, a laptop lost, a virus detected, a confidential document sent to the wrong recipient, a suspicious login.

Any employee who notices or suspects an incident reports it within 30 minutes to the response team, CERT-Ex. It can be reached day and night on internal number 4242, or at the dedicated email address shown on the intranet. One report too many is better than one report missed: no blame is ever placed on whoever raises the alert.

While waiting for CERT-Ex to respond, the suspect workstation is disconnected from the network, cable unplugged and Wi-Fi turned off, but it is not shut down: its RAM holds traces that are useful to the investigation. Do not delete the suspicious email, and do not try to clean the machine yourself.

CERT-Ex classifies each incident on 3 severity levels. A level 1 incident is handled within the day by the support team. A level 2 incident mobilises CERT-Ex, which informs the chief information security officer within the hour. A level 3 incident triggers the crisis unit, convened in under 2 hours and chaired by the chief executive officer.

When an incident affects personal data, the data protection officer is notified without delay: the supervisory authority must be notified no later than 72 hours after the breach is discovered.

Each level 2 or 3 incident gives rise to a written lessons-learned report, drafted within 15 working days, which describes the timeline, the cause and the measures taken to prevent it from happening again. These reports are presented to the security committee, which meets every quarter.
