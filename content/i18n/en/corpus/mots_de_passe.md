# Exemplia password policy

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: these rules describe no real company. -->

At Exemplia, a user account password has at least 14 characters. Length matters more than complexity: a passphrase of four or five unrelated words is recommended, for example a series of randomly drawn words separated by hyphens. Administration accounts, for their part, require at least 20 characters, and their password is generated by the operations team's vault.

A password no longer expires on a fixed date. It is changed only if a compromise is suspected, if it appears in a public data leak, or if an administrator who knew it leaves. In these cases, the change must take place within 12 hours of the security team's alert.

The official password manager is called Coffrix. It is installed on every workstation, and every employee stores their work passwords in it. It is forbidden to write a password down on paper, in a text file or in the mailbox, and to reuse a work password on a personal website.

Two-factor authentication is mandatory for email, remote access and all online tools. The second factor is the ExempliaPass app on the work phone; a physical security key is issued to administrators and to members of the executive committee.

After 5 failed login attempts, an account is locked for 15 minutes. On the third lockout in the same day, the account stays blocked until the support desk steps in, reachable on extension 3100.

An automatic check compares each new password with a list of 100,000 common or already leaked passwords: a password on that list is refused, even if it has more than 14 characters.

IT support never asks for a password, whether by phone or by email. Any such request is a phishing attempt, to be reported immediately.
