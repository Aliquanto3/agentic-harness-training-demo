# Exemplia remote work agreement

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this agreement describes no real company. -->

Any employee on a permanent contract may work remotely up to 3 days a week, after an onboarding period of 2 months. Interns and work-study students are limited to 1 day a week, to keep in touch with their mentor.

Remote work days are declared in the HR tool by the previous Friday at the latest. Tuesday is team day: everyone is on site that day, except in case of absence or business travel. A manager may request exceptional attendance with 48 hours' notice.

Exemplia pays an allowance of €2.50 per day worked remotely, up to 55 euros a month. It covers part of the electricity, heating and internet connection. Equipment is provided on request: a 27-inch screen, a keyboard, a mouse and a headset. An ergonomic chair can be lent on the advice of the occupational health service.

The workstation connects to Exemplia's network through the company VPN as soon as it is off site. Working on a public Wi-Fi network, on a train, in a café or in a hotel, is allowed only with the VPN on and a privacy filter fitted to the screen.

Remote work from abroad is limited to 20 days per calendar year, with the written agreement of the manager and of the human resources department, for tax and social security reasons. It remains forbidden from any country on the list of high-risk countries kept by the security team.

Availability hours are the same as at the office: from 9:30 am to 12 noon and from 2 pm to 5 pm. Outside these hours, the right to disconnect applies: no email sent after 8 pm calls for a reply before the next day.

The agreement is revised every 3 years with the staff representatives. The next revision is planned for the autumn.
