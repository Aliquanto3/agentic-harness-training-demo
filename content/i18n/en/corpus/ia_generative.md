# Exemplia's generative AI use charter

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this charter describes no real company. -->

Exemplia encourages the use of generative artificial intelligence to save time on writing, summarising and translating, in line with this charter. It applies to chat assistants, image generators and code completion tools.

The reference tool is ExempliaGPT, an assistant hosted in Exemplia's data centre: the texts entrusted to it do not leave the company and are not used to train any model. It accepts information up to the Confidential level. Secret-level information is entrusted to no AI tool, not even an internal one.

Public AI services, accessed from a browser, receive only Public-level information. It is forbidden to paste into them a contract, an extract of Exemplia's source code, personal data or the minutes of an internal meeting.

Content produced by an AI is always reviewed by a human before being shared, and the person who shares it remains responsible for it. A document intended for a client that was largely written by an AI carries the statement “Written with the help of an AI tool”. Figures, quotations and legal references suggested by an AI are checked at the source, because a model can invent them.

Before getting access to ExempliaGPT, each employee takes a 3-hour online training course, then passes a quiz with at least 8 correct answers out of 10. A 45-minute refresher is mandatory every year.

The AI committee, made up of 6 members from security, legal, human resources and the business units, reviews each new tool before it is adopted, within 6 weeks. A tool request is submitted on the intranet, with the use case and the level of the information processed.
