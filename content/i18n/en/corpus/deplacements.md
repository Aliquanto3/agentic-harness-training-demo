# Business travel and expense claims at Exemplia

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: these rates describe no real company. -->

All business travel is approved in advance by the manager in the Voyagex booking tool, which automatically applies the rules below. A booking made outside Voyagex is reimbursed only as an exception, with a written justification.

The train is mandatory for any journey of less than 4 and a half hours, in second class. First class is allowed for a journey of more than 3 hours booked the day before departure or on the day itself, when it costs no more than 20% above second class. Air travel is allowed only beyond 4 and a half hours by train, in economy class, and business class is reserved for flights of more than 7 hours.

A hotel night is reimbursed up to 130 euros in Paris and 95 euros in other cities, breakfast included. A meal taken while travelling is reimbursed up to 22 euros; a meal with a client is reimbursed on invoice, up to 60 euros per person, with the name of the guest company.

Using a personal vehicle entitles the employee to mileage allowances at the current scale, on a route calculated by Voyagex. Taxis and chauffeur-driven cars are reimbursed only when there is no public transport, or after 10 pm.

Expense claims are entered in the NotaFrais app no later than 30 days after the expense, with a photo of each receipt. After 90 days, an expense is no longer reimbursed. The reimbursement is paid with the salary of the month following the manager's approval.

For a trip of more than 5 days, an expense advance may be requested, up to 800 euros. It is settled on the first expense claim after the return.
