# Personal data retention periods at Exemplia

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: these periods describe no real company and do not constitute legal advice. -->

Personal data is kept only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. Exemplia's record of processing activities sets, for each processing activity, an active period, during which departments use the data, then an archiving period, during which only the legal department can access it.

Unsuccessful job applications are kept for 2 years after the last contact with the applicant, who is informed of this and may ask for them to be deleted sooner. Data on prospects who never replied is deleted after 3 years without contact. Client data is kept for the whole contractual relationship, then archived for 5 years after the contract ends.

Application login logs are kept for 12 months, CCTV recordings of the premises for 30 days, and deactivated access badges are erased 3 months after the employee leaves. Payslips are kept in electronic form for 5 years by the payroll department.

An automatic purge runs every quarter, on the first Monday of January, April, July and October. It deletes or anonymises the data whose period has expired, and produces a report sent to the data protection officer. A department that needs to keep data longer, for example for an ongoing dispute, must obtain a written exemption from the legal department before the purge.

Anyone can exercise their rights of access, rectification and erasure by writing to the data protection officer. Exemplia replies within one month, which can be extended by 2 months for a complex request, in which case it informs the person.

Working copies, such as spreadsheet extracts or files sent by email, follow the same periods as the original processing activity: it is up to each person to delete them.
