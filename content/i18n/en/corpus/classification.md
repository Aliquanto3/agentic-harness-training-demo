# Information classification at Exemplia

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this policy describes no real company. -->

All information produced or received by Exemplia carries one of the following 4 classification levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Secret. By default, an unmarked document is treated as Internal.

The Public level covers what can be published without harm: sales brochures, job offers, press releases. The Internal level covers day-to-day operations: internal memos, organisation charts, procedures. The Confidential level protects anything whose disclosure would harm Exemplia or its clients: contracts, personal data, financial results before publication. The Secret level is reserved for a short list of strategic information, such as acquisition projects or detailed business continuity plans.

The level is chosen by the document's author and shown in the footer of every page, as well as in the subject line of emails, in square brackets, for example “[CONFIDENTIAL]”. The information owner, usually the head of department, may raise or lower this level.

A Confidential document leaves Exemplia's network only encrypted, through the secure sharing tool Transvault; the sharing link expires after 7 days. A Secret document is never sent by email: it can be consulted only in the data room, on a workstation with no internet connection, and each consultation is entered in a signed register.

Confidential and Secret paper documents are stored in a locked cabinet and destroyed with a level P-5 shredder. Printouts at these levels go through secure printing: the document is released only once the badge is presented at the printer.

Every year, in January, the head of each department reviews the classification of the 20 most consulted documents in their scope. A classification error is not misconduct, but Secret information disclosed by mistake must be reported as a security incident.
