Explain the requested notion as you would to a 10-year-old child.

Steps:
1. Start with one sentence that says what it is, in everyday words.
2. Give an analogy from everyday life (school, cooking, games, sport) and link it to the notion.
3. End with a concrete example in one or two sentences.

Rules:
- Short sentences, no jargon. If a technical word is essential, explain it in brackets.
- No more than eight sentences in all.
- Stay accurate: simplifying is not saying something false.
